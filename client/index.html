          Voices and recitations

          Voices and recitations are synthesized with artificial intelligence.

          Haunt introductions (Morrow, Nix, Vesper, Raksha) are fixed fictional lines produced with the OpenAI text-to-speech API; they do not describe real play history. Any further haunt speech delivered by the game server is synthesized the same way.

          The Hanuman Chalisa (attributed to Tulsidas), the Shiva Tandava Stotram (conventionally attributed to Ravana) and the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra (Rigveda 7.59.12) are spoken recitations of traditional public-domain texts, synthesized for this project with the OpenAI speech API. They are not human or commercial recordings, imitate no named artist, and their pronunciation has not been reviewed by a fluent speaker. Provenance notes with the exact source texts ship beside each file.

          Imagery

          The haunt sprites, deity portraits and the title backdrop are images synthesized with artificial intelligence for this project. Haunts are drawn as flat camera-facing sprites in the 3D courtyard, not rigged 3D characters.

          3D assets

          Haunted house from Babylon.js Assets (CC BY 4.0, unmodified). Graveyard props, lanterns and pumpkins from Kenney's Graveyard Kit (CC0). KayKit Halloween Bits (CC0) ship in the asset folder but are not placed in this scene.
