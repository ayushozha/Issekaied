# Issekaied faceoff screen concepts

These are illustrative screen/key-art concepts, not gameplay screenshots. Five distinct compositions generated from our project cover.

## 01-moonlit-graveyard-faceoff

Create ONE exceptional standalone landscape16:9 ISSEKAIED Halloween game screen illustration, using the supplied cover only for exact character identities and refined painterly detail. The Returned is the hooded ochre-coated adult hunter with bronze lantern and tuning-fork relic. The benevolent protective pantheon is Ganesha, Hanuman, Shiva and Vishnu, with respectful recognizable iconography, aiding the hunter, NEVER enemies. Antagonists are Morrow (hood,bells,cracked ivory mask), Nix (small charming collector spirit in strapped antique chest), and Vesper (moth burial wings and skull mask). CRITICAL: this must be a real faceoff: the hero side and enemy side face EACH OTHER, with bodies, faces, weapons, gaze and gesture directed across a visible confrontation axis. DO NOT pose everyone facing the camera or in the same direction. All four deities should be recognizable as integrated luminous guardians behind the hero, facing the antagonists. Beautiful coherent scene rather than collage, distinct readable silhouettes, atmospheric dark material detail, restrained light, no glare/no gore/no UI/buttons/logos/watermark. This is screen concept art, not a gameplay screenshot. MOONLIT GRAVEYARD DUEL. Wide low side-on camera. Hero occupies left third in right-facing profile, thrusting lantern forward. Four divine guardians rise behind on left, oriented right. Morrow,Vesper and Nix occupy right third, turned LEFT toward the hunter. Empty charged space between factions creates strong two-sided confrontation. Blue full moon, wet cemetery path, ruined Bellgrave arch, ravens and sparse amber jack-o-lanterns. Dark navy, silver mist and antique gold. Large elegant title ISSEKAIED in upper-center sky, no other text. Designed as a dramatic main-menu cover.
