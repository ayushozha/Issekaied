# Rebirth prologue

`rebirth.mp4` is a 10.000-second, 1280×720 H.264/AAC animated-art video rendered for Issekaied. It pans across `assets/generated/rebirth-prologue.png`, with gentle opening/closing fades. The traveler leaves a fictional modern world and awakens in Bellgrave; no actual death footage or human recording is used.

Narration, synthesized with artificial intelligence through OpenAI's speech API using the configured Source voice: “Your old world ended. But death was only a doorway. You awaken in Bellgrave. The dead remember. This time, fight back.” The narration was accelerated 1.1× to fit the ten-second timeline, padded and faded. Source and model selection came from deployment environment configuration.

The built-in image-generation tool produced the original artwork. Prompt:

> Create a cinematic dark fantasy game prologue storyboard as ONE 16:9 widescreen illustration, clean single composition with a fluid transition from left to right, no panels or borders and absolutely NO TEXT or logos. Left half: rainy modern city street at night, a young adult fully clothed traveler in a dark hooded jacket falling gently into an abstract pool of shadow and red autumn leaves, eyes closed, an implied end of a previous life without wounds or gore or visible impact. Center: the silhouette dissolves into a vertical golden lantern light portal. Right half: the SAME traveler awakens on one knee wearing the ragged black-and-gold coat of a first-person ghost hunter, holding an ornate gold lantern, in an incredible haunted gothic cathedral cemetery at Halloween, red stained glass, hanging webs, candles, pumpkins, pale spectral enemies observing from the far shadows. Emotional arc: death becomes rebirth in Bellgrave. Rich painterly AAA game key art, deep crimson, gold, cold midnight blue, readable silhouettes, cinematic volumetric lighting, richly textured stone and cloth. This art will be panned across for a 10-second rebirth intro in the game Issekaied; keep the traveler recognizable on both halves. Art only, no typography, no UI, no watermarks.
