## Optimized browser audio derivatives

The Ogg files in this directory are derivatives of the corresponding MP3 files listed above. Re-encoded to stereo Ogg Opus at 96 kbps (title, hub, exploration, tension, scare) or 128 kbps (combat, boss, Danse Macabre), resampled to 48 kHz, with attached cover images removed. Original recordings remain unchanged. No musical excerpts, new performances or Hanuman Chalisa recording were added. Original attribution is preserved above; this copy records processing changes.
